# fizz_dr_scripts
Ruby scripts for Lich5 for the game known as DragonRealms

This is the `scripts/custom` folder of a Lich5 DragonRealms install, for Fizzlestiks. Run any of them as `;name`.

## Stashing in the vault

Both scripts use your vault at your hometown's Carousel (Crossing is `;go2 8285`). They walk there, go through the first free booth (blackwood, ironwood, then rosewood; the platinum arch is restricted for Fizzlestiks, and if all three booths are busy they wait), `PULL LEVER`, `GO DOOR` and `OPEN VAULT`. If you're already in the vault chamber (where `trash-sort` leaves you) they start right there, vault open or not, and from a booth they start at the lever, since the map can't walk you out of either. When they're done, or if anything goes wrong on the way, they close the vault and leave you in the Carousel. If rent is due they stop at the arch: pay at the Carousel desk (`GO DESK`, 5,000 Kronars for 30 days).

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `stash.lic` | Puts things in your vault so your containers have room. `;stash crafting` (forging, shaping, enchanting, outfitting and alchemy gear: the yaml's `forging_tools`, `shaping_tools`, `enchanting_tools`, `outfitting_tools` and `alchemy_tools` plus crafting books, logbooks, oil, flux and the mana fount; and the materials each one uses, found with LOOK IN across your `storage_containers`: ingots, nuggets, metal bars and ore; lumber, lumber deeds, bone stacks, stone deeds and loose stone; beads and metal shards (sigil-scrolls stay with you, since artificing orders use the ones in your bag before buying more); leather, cloth of any kind (steelsilk, electroweave, Imperial weave, titanese and the rest, but not a chamois, cleaning or polishing cloth), yarn, thread and dyes. Coal nuggets go down the vault's garbage chute instead of into the vault: remedy and taskmaster buy one whenever none is on hand, and the marathon never uses one (a gold nugget is stashed as usual). A material whose name GET doesn't take as its last two words, like a thin Imperial weave cloth, is tried by its other words and then its noun, keeping only a material that's being stashed), `;stash crafting forging` (the same as `;stash forging`; t2's crafting blocks end with it), `;stash forging`, `;stash shaping`, `;stash enchanting`, `;stash outfitting`, `;stash alchemy`, `;stash materials` (just the materials, no tools: the heavy part; `;stash materials forging` and so on for one discipline's), `;stash weapons` (for when you're not hunting: every weapon in the yaml's `weapon_training`, taken from wherever the yaml's `gear` list says it's kept, the way combat-trainer gets it: worn ones taken off, others off the harness or out of the sheath or rucksack), `;stash extras` (just your extras), `;stash all` (crafting gear and materials plus your extras), `;stash add <item>` (taps it to check the name, then adds it to your extras), `;stash remove <item>`, `;stash list`. If the vault fills up, the rest stays where it is. |
| `unstash.lic` | Gets back everything `stash` put away, and only that, checking each item's full name so other things in your vault stay put. Each thing goes back in the container it came from. `;unstash forging`, `;unstash shaping`, `;unstash enchanting`, `;unstash outfitting`, `;unstash alchemy`, `;unstash crafting` (all five), `;unstash weapons` or `;unstash extras` gets back just that part, since `stash` writes down what each thing is for. Those leave crafting materials in the vault, since they're heavy: `;unstash materials` (or `;unstash materials forging` and so on) gets them, and plain `;unstash` gets everything. `;unstash marathon`, which `start_marathon` runs before `;taskmaster marathon`, gets the outfitting gear plus the burlap cloth, rat-pelt leather, wool yarn and cotton thread the marathon sews and knits from (not your rare cloths and leathers), so it doesn't buy them again. When a name fits more than one stashed thing ("pewter bar" for the tiny and the huge one), whichever GET hands over goes home as that one if it's also being unstashed, and the name is asked again. `;unstash crafting forging` (or shaping, enchanting, outfitting, alchemy) is the same as `;unstash forging`, tools only: each of Fizzlestiks's t2 crafting blocks starts with it and ends with `;stash crafting forging`, so the work orders always have their logbook and tools. When none of that discipline's tools are in the vault it does nothing, without walking there. Weapons go back the way combat-trainer puts them away after a hunt: worn again if your `standard` gear set wears them, else on the harness, in their container or stowed. They're fetched by their gear-list name ("dwarven.voulge"), without the full-name check. |
| `trash-sort.lic` | Clears the cheap things out of a full vault. Opens it the same way, lists it with `RUMMAGE VAULT` (LOOK IN VAULT stops partway through a full vault with "a lot of other stuff", so it's only used if RUMMAGE gives no list), takes each thing out in turn and APPRAISEs it. It asks by noun, the way you would by hand: `get deed from vault`, then `get second deed from vault` after one it keeps, and so on (RUMMAGE's long names like "deed for a lelori log" don't work for GET, and nor do sizes like "tiny"), and every copy is appraised once, since two tiny brass bars can be worth different amounts: under 10,000 Kronars goes down the vault's garbage chute and off `stash`'s list, anything else goes back. Never thrown out, or even picked up: crafting tools and supplies (the yaml's `*_tools`, what `stash` put away as tools, and books, logbooks, instructions, oil, flux, founts and sigil-scrolls, which APPRAISE puts at 62 Kronars though the shop sells them at 625); your weapons and gear; the burlap cloth, rat-pelt leather, wool yarn and cotton thread that `;unstash marathon` gets out; and anything the sorter files as crafting gear, stacker, trainer, container or box. Crafting materials aren't tools, so they're appraised like everything else: ingots, bars, lumps, fragments, shards, deeds, stacks, stones, beads, leather, cloth, yarn, thread and dyes (a cleaning, chamois or polishing cloth counts as a tool). Also kept after appraising: containers, and anything without an exact Kronar figure ("worth exactly nothing", as memory orbs appraise; "probably worth around several thousand"; another currency). Anything already in `trash-sort.yaml` as kept isn't appraised again, copy for copy: with two huge selatila shards kept before and three in the vault, only the third one GET reaches is. What was thrown out before is appraised again each time, since it's a different one and may be worth more. It leaves you in the vault chamber with the vault open, since the chute only empties when you leave: check it for anything you want, then `CLOSE VAULT` and go. |

All three keep their data in Lich's `data/DR/<character>/` folder: `stash.yaml` is what's in the vault right now and where it came from, `stash-extras.yaml` is your extras list, and `trash-sort.yaml` is what `trash-sort` last appraised each thing at, and what each kept copy is worth.

## Other scripts

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `sell-gems.lic` | Walks to the nearest gem shop, sells every loose gem from your rucksack and scavenger's belt (or containers you name), and walks back. |
| `empty-sacks.lic` | Empties the random-material sacks in your tote: takes each one out, opens it, moves everything into your tote with `;transfer-items` (your rucksack once the tote is full), and drops the empty sack for the janitor. Start with empty hands. If both are full it stops with the sack in your hand. |
| `drop-junk.lic` | Walks to room 8246 (or `;drop-junk <room> ...`) and gets rid of junk: each piece goes in a bin if the room has one, else on the ground, and `DUMP JUNK` calls the janitor. With no items named it takes the crafting leftovers from your crafting_container: unfinished thrusting blades and botched or marred reinforced netting, checking each one's full name so a finished blade or good netting goes back. Netting instructions are kept, since Zasele may stop replacing lost ones. `;drop-junk kasai pasaba` takes every kasai and pasaba from your crafting_container and storage_containers, whatever its full name (use a dot for two words: `steel.kasai`). `--safe` drops everything on the ground and skips `DUMP JUNK`, so you can LOOK, pick up anything that should stay, and DUMP JUNK yourself. |
| `smash-shells-heal.lic` | Fork of `smash-shells` that goes to a healer and waits for a heal when you're hurt, then goes back to smashing shells. |
| `raffle-watch.lic` | Plays the festival raffle and reports to the Familiar window. When picks close, it posts your odds from the number of tickets torn off. Posts each winner as their name goes up on the result board. If it's you, it beeps and puts your ticket on the counter to claim the prize; if you lose, it puts the ticket in the bucket; when picks reopen, it gets a new ticket. Runs until killed; `;raffle-watch once` stops after one drawing. |
| `announce_monitor.lic` | Posts the Hollow Eve festival's raffle, Limited Treasures and creature capture announcements to a Discord webhook so they reach your phone, with a reminder of how to join (`;go2 raffle`, `;go2 10205` for the Limited Treasures lounge, `;capture-critter hollow_eve`), skipping chatter in the raffle room and the shop. It also posts when the game connection drops, even in the middle of a hunt, since you'll miss announcements until you log back in (not when you exit, quit, a script like 2hourquit or afk exits for you, you close Saga, idle out or log in elsewhere); that alert keeps retrying for up to an hour in case your internet is what dropped. Autostart it with `;autostart add announce_monitor`. `;announce_monitor setup` sets the webhook and which alerts to send; `;announce_monitor test` sends a test message, and `;announce_monitor test drop` a test disconnect alert. The webhook lives in Lich's `data/announce_monitor.yaml`, not in this repo. |
| `LT.lic` | Buys at Gortik's Limited Treasures the moment the sale opens (Lich version of Lycette's Genie `limited` scripts). Start it in the lounge: when the deathfiber curtain powers down it goes through the curtained door, buys what you name in that order (`;LT mask greatcoat bookbag`) or whatever is third (`;LT third`; the items sit on three desks, displays or stands depending on the sale, so it tries each, and if it finds none it buys what the clerk is handing other people; `;LT third table` looks on tables first), then echoes all three items. `;LT` alone just looks; `;LT now ...` is only for when the sale has already opened. It never buys the rock. |
| `2hourquit.lic` | Exits the game two hours after it starts, or after the minutes you give it (`;2hourquit 90`). |
| `start_marathon.lic` | Starts `;taskmaster marathon` with the outfitting tools, and the stock you already have, on hand. When `stash` left any of either in the vault, it runs `;unstash marathon` first (the tools, plus the burlap cloth, rat-pelt leather, wool yarn and cotton thread the marathon uses; your rare cloths and leathers stay). Then it combines the loose pieces of each stock in your crafting_container into one, since taskmaster COUNTs only one piece of each before buying more. It starts the marathon only once none of the tools are left in the vault, since taskmaster doesn't check Zasele's tools before buying stock and paying for a task; stock that couldn't be got back doesn't stop it. When nothing for the marathon is stashed there's no trip to the vault. Unstash needs empty hands; if yours are full and tools are stashed it stops and says so. Fizzlestiks's `marathon` alias runs it, and anything typed after `marathon` goes on to taskmaster; typing `;taskmaster marathon` yourself skips it. |
| `stop_marathon.lic` | Ends a running `;taskmaster marathon` (or `repeat`/`cycle`) after its next hand-in, once the reward sack is sorted, instead of in the middle of a task. It sets taskmaster's own "Finish your tasks." flag without saying anything aloud. `;stop_marathon cancel` keeps it going. |
